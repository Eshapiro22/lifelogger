About

LifeLogger is your personal space to capture moments, track habits, and reflect on the journey that makes you you. Whether it's daily journal entries, milestone celebrations, or simple gratitude notes — everything lives here.

Built with the belief that small, consistent reflections lead to meaningful growth over time.

Features

📝

Daily Journal

Write freely about your day, your thoughts, and your feelings. No judgment, just expression.

📊

Habit Tracking

Set goals and track your habits over time. Watch your streaks grow and celebrate consistency.

🎯

Milestone Markers

Flag the big moments — promotions, travels, personal wins — so you never forget how far you've come.

🔒

Private & Secure

Your data stays yours. No ads, no tracking, no sharing. Just a safe space for self-reflection.
