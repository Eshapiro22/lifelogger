The CSV export of your territory. Columns used: Account Name, Type, Active Customer, Vertical, Website, Billing City/State.

2. LinkedIn data export (recommended)

LinkedIn → Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data. Pick Connections and Messages. It's the most reliable source: exact company names, connect dates, and full message history including who replied.

3. Page captures

Use the toolbar popup on My Network › Connections, Sales Nav lead lists, LinkedIn Messaging, and Sales Nav Inbox. Captures add to what's imported above.
